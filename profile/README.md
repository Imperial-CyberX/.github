## About Us

Imperial CyberX is a cybersecurity and technology engineering enterprise focused on building secure, reliable, and practical digital solutions.

Our work spans cybersecurity, software development, cloud infrastructure, network engineering, and emerging technologies.

We believe technology should solve real problems. Every project begins with understanding the challenge, evaluating the available approaches, and developing solutions that balance security, performance, and long-term reliability.

Our GitHub organization brings together our engineering activities, selected development projects, technical resources, and research initiatives.

## What We Do

### Cybersecurity Engineering
We explore practical approaches to vulnerability assessment, network security, intrusion detection, threat analysis, and secure system development.

### Software Development
Our engineering interests include web applications, business management platforms, automation tools, and software systems designed with usability, maintainability, and security in mind.

### Cloud & Infrastructure
We work with cloud technologies, infrastructure management, secure deployment practices, and modern application architectures.

### Network Engineering
Our technical activities include network analysis, monitoring, infrastructure security, and secure communication systems.

### AI & Emerging Technologies
We investigate how artificial intelligence, machine learning, and automation can support engineering workflows and address security challenges.

## ICX LAB | Research & Development

ICX LAB is the research and experimental engineering initiative of Imperial CyberX.

Our research interests include digital forensics, network traffic analysis, intrusion detection systems, cybersecurity automation, and intelligent security technologies.

Through ICX LAB, we aim to explore new ideas, develop working prototypes, evaluate technical methods, and document meaningful research findings.

Selected projects and research implementations will be made available through our GitHub repositories when they are ready for public release.
